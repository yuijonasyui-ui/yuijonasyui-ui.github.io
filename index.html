Vice Snippets

The best GTA 6 stream moments, as YouTube Shorts.

Vice Snippets is a YouTube channel (@snippetsvice) that publishes short highlights from Twitch streamers playing GTA 6. We only use clips from streamers who have given us permission, and every video credits the streamer by name with a link to their channel.

About the Vice Snippets app

The Vice Snippets app is an internal tool operated by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos to the Vice Snippets channel only. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other user's YouTube account or data.

Streamers who want a clip removed can contact us and it will be taken down the same day.
