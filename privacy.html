Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes how the Vice Snippets app ("the app") handles data. The app is an internal tool used by the owner of the Vice Snippets YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • The channel owner's YouTube account: permission to upload videos and to read basic channel information (channel name and ID) so the app can confirm it is uploading to the right channel.
  • Public Twitch clip information (clip title, view count, streamer name) from the Twitch API, for streamers who have given permission.

The app does not access, collect or store data about YouTube viewers or any other YouTube users.

How data is used and stored

Channel information is used only to upload videos to the Vice Snippets channel. The OAuth token that grants upload access is stored securely on the server that runs the app and is never shared with third parties. Clip information is stored in a private database to avoid posting the same clip twice.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Video files are sent to YouTube for upload, and clip transcripts may be processed by an AI service to write video titles; no personal user data is included.

Revoking access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. On request we delete all stored data related to the app within 7 days.
